## Question

How does the probability quality of the frozen Phase 2 Elo model (and the ranking baseline) compare with de-vigged
bookmaker probabilities on identical ATP Grand Slam main-draw matches?
- This is **not** a test of a trading or betting signal.
- It makes **no** profitability claim.

## Timing

- **Decision time:** the earliest scheduled start the provider ever showed for the event, minus **60 minutes**.
  Anchoring on the earliest start means a later reschedule, which is only learned later, cannot move the decision.
- **Quote admissibility (all required):**
  - observed at or before the decision, and before the start;
  - source timestamp (`last_update`) present and no later than both the observation and the decision;
  - no blocking timing flag;
  - both sides from the same bookmaker and the same observation.
- **Freshness:** primary limit **30 minutes** (decision time minus source time). Sensitivity at **15 and 60 minutes**.
- **Missing source timestamp:** rejected.

**Information asymmetry (stated in advance):**
- Elo features use the conservative day-bound cutoff: the earliest instant of the earliest possible match day.
- Market quotes may be observed up to 60 minutes before the scheduled start.
- The market therefore has strictly more information, including earlier-round results that the model may not be
  allowed to see.
- Any difference describes probability quality under unequal information and is **not** evidence of a trading
  signal in either direction.

## Market references

- **Primary:**
  - Pinnacle (`pinnacle`, EU region), *if* it has an available quote for **≥ 80 %** of the linked population at the
    primary timing.
  - Otherwise, the bookmaker with the highest availability (ties alphabetical).
  - The choice is made **before** outcomes are joined, using availability only. It is enforced by
    `choose_reference` and the outcome-blind guards.
- **Secondary:** the median of proportionally de-vigged probabilities across bookmakers with an available quote
  (minimum 2), labelled **non-executable**.
- **De-vig:** proportional is primary. Power and Shin are reported as sensitivity. The method is not chosen by
  outcomes.
- **Executability:** all prices are observed quotes. No claim is made that any stake would have been accepted.

## Metrics

- **Primary:** paired per-match **log-loss** and **Brier** differences for each contrast, as model − reference
  (negative favours the model):
  1. Elo − primary market;
  2. ranking baseline − primary market;
  3. Elo − consensus.
- **Uncertainty:** block bootstrap over ISO weeks of tournament start (2,000 replicates, seed 20261102, 95 %).
  Every match of one edition shares one start date, so the pilot has only **10 blocks** (one per edition). The
  intervals are coarse, and this is reported.
- **Always reported:**
  - sample sizes at every stage;
  - link-status counts;
  - population without a linked event;
  - selection-status counts;
  - bookmaker availability;
  - quote-age quantiles;
  - calibration (intercept, slope, ECE) and reliability bins;
  - the staleness and de-vig sensitivities.
- **Population rule:** every contrast is scored only on matches where **all** compared columns exist (identical
  population). Drop counts are reported.

## Conduct

- One confirmatory run. A re-run is allowed only to fix a documented bug, and every run is registered with the
  protocol hash.
- Nothing is tuned against outcomes: no bookmaker, de-vig method, timing or staleness threshold.
- The analysis code is fixed now (`quantedge.odds.pilot`) and tested on synthetic data.
